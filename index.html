Curious why the numbers land where they do?

When you first pick, there's a 1 in 3 chance you picked the car and a 2 in 3 chance the car is behind one of the other two doors. The host always opens a door with a goat — that doesn't change where the car actually is, but it does concentrate that 2-in-3 chance onto the one remaining unopened door. Switching lets you take that 2-in-3 door instead of your original 1-in-3 pick.
